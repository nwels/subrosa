SUB ROSA
before you proceed, understand this:

what is spoken here is not meant to be traced.

you may remain unnamed, or choose to be seen.

once submitted, your confession cannot be undone.

every entry will be visible for 120 days before entering the archive — where it will remain.

this space allows expression without identity, but not without consequence.

this is where you can share everything you want, no moderation, no judgement, no trace.
